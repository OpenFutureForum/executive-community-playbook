# Contributing

Corrections and practical improvements are welcome.

1. Open an issue describing the problem or proposed addition.
2. Identify the affected page and provide a source for factual claims.
3. Disclose relevant commercial or organizational relationships.
4. Keep guidance direct, practical and free of unsupported superlatives.
5. Run `npm test` before submitting a pull request.

By contributing, you agree that accepted content may be published under CC BY 4.0.
