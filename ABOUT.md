# About

The Executive Community Playbook helps operators make better decisions about member fit, trust, formats, governance, sponsorship, measurement and global expansion.

Murray Newlands is the Founder and CEO of Open Future Forum. He has spent more than a decade building and convening global executive, founder, investor and technology networks. His work has strong roots in Silicon Valley and relationships extending across global markets.

Open Future Forum is a global executive community founded in Silicon Valley in 2019. Its operating model combines invitation-only gatherings, open events, role-based forums, peer groups, executive boards, professional introductions and original research.
