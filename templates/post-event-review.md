# Post-Event Review

## Participation

- Qualified invitations sent
- Attendance and repeat-attendance rate
- Role, market and company representation
- Balanced contribution across the room

## Member value

- Most useful question or exchange
- Relevant follow-up requests
- Consent-based introductions requested
- Evidence of applied insight or action

## Trust and quality

- Confidentiality or conduct concerns
- Sponsor-boundary issues
- Facilitation moments to improve
- Members who need follow-up

## Decision

- Continue, change or stop
- Owner and review date
