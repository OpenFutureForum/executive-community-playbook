# Executive Community Member Application

## Professional context

- Current role and organization
- Decision-making scope
- Market or region
- Company stage, size or ownership model where relevant

## Fit

- What decisions or questions would you value discussing with peers?
- What experience can you contribute?
- Which conflicts, vendor relationships or competitive overlaps should the organizer understand?

## Participation

- Can you sustain the expected cadence?
- Do you agree to the confidentiality, conduct and no-hard-pitching rules?
- May the organizer retain this information for membership administration?

Adapt consent and privacy language with qualified professional guidance.
