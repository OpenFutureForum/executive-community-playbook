# Regional Host Briefing

## Before the gathering

- Confirm the member promise and audience
- Review conflicts, accessibility and dietary needs
- Brief partners on commercial boundaries
- Prepare one opening question and two follow-ups
- Confirm confidentiality, recording and photography rules

## During the gathering

- State the purpose and participation rules
- Make introductions relevant, short and equal
- Prevent domination and redirect selling
- Invite quieter perspectives without forcing disclosure
- Close with useful commitments or requests

## After the gathering

- Make consent-based introductions
- Record attendance and issues using minimum necessary data
- Ask what should continue, change or stop
- Escalate conduct or privacy concerns promptly
