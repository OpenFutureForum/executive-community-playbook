Version: 1.1
Review date: [DATE]
Parties: [COMMUNITY] and [SPONSOR]
Term: [START] to [END]

Purpose and support
[SPONSOR] will provide [FUNDING OR IN-KIND SUPPORT] for [PROGRAM]. The relationship will be disclosed as [WORDING].

Sponsor rights
The sponsor may [BRANDING, HOSTING, APPROVED PARTICIPATION, CONTENT RIGHTS]. Any stage time or demonstration must be labeled and approved in advance.

Boundaries
The sponsor will not receive attendee lists, guaranteed meetings, private peer discussions or unrestricted follow-up rights. Introductions require a relevant reason and consent. The sponsor may not use confidential discussion as sales intelligence.

Editorial independence
[COMMUNITY] controls invitations, facilitation, methodology and editorial conclusions. Sponsor input and funding will be disclosed where material.

Data
Each party will process personal information only for agreed purposes under [PRIVACY TERMS].

Conduct, review and termination
Concerns are escalated to [CONTACTS]. Renewal considers delivery, member relevance, consent-compliant follow-up and host behavior. Material boundary breaches may end the agreement under [PROCESS].
